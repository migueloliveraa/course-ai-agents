# Investment Funds Summary

## 1. Fidelity Blue Chip Growth Fund  
**Objective:** Seeks growth of capital over the long term.  
**Strategy:** Invests at least 80% of assets in blue-chip companies, focusing on companies with above-average earnings growth potential.  
This fund aims to provide consistent capital appreciation and is suitable for investors looking for long-term growth aligned with market trends.  

## 2. Vanguard Total Stock Market Index Fund  
**Objective:** Track the performance of the CRSP US Total Market Index, measuring the investment return of the overall U.S. stock market.  
**Strategy:** Provides exposure to large-, mid-, and small-cap stocks diversified across growth and value styles.  
This index fund is designed for those wanting broad exposure to the U.S. equity market, emphasizing passive investment strategies.  

## 3. Fidelity 500 Index Fund  
**Objective:** Seek to provide investment results that correspond to the total return performance of the S&P 500 Index.  
**Strategy:** A well-diversified, market-cap-weighted portfolio of 500 of the largest U.S. stocks.  
This fund is ideal for investors looking for a low-cost way to invest in large-cap U.S. equities, closely mirroring the performance of the market.  

---  
This summary highlights the goals and visions of three prominent investment funds, showcasing their commitment to long-term growth and market tracking.
